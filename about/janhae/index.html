Artist profile · JAN

Jan / Janhae

Ployshompoo Supasap (พลอยชมพู ศุภทรัพย์), professionally listed by GMMTV as JAN and widely known as Jan / Janhae, is a Thai actress and singer.

View Activities & Credits ↗

Profile

GMMTV lists her artist name as JAN. Her official artist profile records her date of birth as 5 January 1995 and height as 170 cm. The public social handles linked from that profile use the name janhae.

Jan and Janhae refer to the same artist, Ployshompoo Supasap — not two separate people.
